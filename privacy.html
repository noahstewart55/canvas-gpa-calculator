Privacy Policy: GPA Calculator for Canvas

Last updated: November 16, 2026

GPA Calculator for Canvas ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that adds a GPA page to the Canvas learning management system. This policy explains what data the extension handles and what it does with it.

What the extension reads

When you open the GPA page, the extension requests your course list and current course scores from your school's Canvas site (the site you are already logged in to, ending in instructure.com). It uses your existing Canvas login in your browser. It never sees or stores your password.

What the extension stores

The extension saves the following on your own computer using Chrome's local extension storage:

  • Which courses you chose to include, the credit hours you entered, and any letter grades you set
  • Your grading-scale choice and, if you enter it, a previous GPA and credit total
  • The address of your school's Canvas site, so the toolbar button can open it

Your course grades are kept in memory only while the page is open and are not saved.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It has no server.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or remotely hosted code.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer your data, and does not use it for any purpose other than showing your GPA.
  • It only runs on Canvas pages (*.instructure.com).

Deleting your data

Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored. You can also clear individual entries by editing them on the GPA page.
